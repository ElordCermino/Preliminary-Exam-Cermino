# Activity 1: Presentation Design Principles

> Discuss the design principles you applied and how they improved your presentation.

![Activity 1](Activity1.png)

---

## What I Created

I created a vintage, rubber-hose style promotional poster for a free coffee event hosted by the Ateneo de Davao University School of Nursing.

## Why I Came Up With the Concept

I wanted an eye-catching, retro-themed design that stands out instantly while clearly delivering event details to students. Combining a friendly character mascot with bold typography creates a playful, inviting tone appropriate for a casual booth event.

## How I Developed My Final Output

I placed a central animated coffee bean mascot holding a cup to serve as the visual anchor. Behind it, I added repeating light typography ("FREE COFFEE") for texture, framed the bottom with high-contrast text blocks containing event details, and incorporated the official school seal for legitimacy.

## Design Principles Applied

Contrast: Dark-colored blocks at the bottom part and light-colored outlined typography are contrasting sharply against the warm orange background.

Hierarchy: The eye is drawn first to the bold "FREE COFFEE" header and central mascot, then down to the catchy tagline, and finally to the specific event details like the date, venue, and "First Come First Serve" basis.

Repetition & Alignment: Repeating background text creates rhythm, while centered alignment creates balance throughout the layout.

Color Mix: A warm, warm-toned palette (brown, beige, orange, dark navy) establishes a unified retro aesthetic.

## How They Improved My Presentation

These principles make the poster visually engaging from a distance while ensuring the essential information, that is what, when, and where, is immediately readable and organized logically for the audience to see.
